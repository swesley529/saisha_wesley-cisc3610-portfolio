What is WellnessGuide?

WellnessGuide is a Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to help you build healthy daily habits. It provides educational content and audio guidance on five key wellness topics: Meditation, Journaling, Exercise, Third Spaces, and Hobbies.

The app can be installed on your device and used anytime — even without an internet connection.

Features

  • 5 wellness topic cards with images and descriptions
  • Audio guidance for each topic
  • Sticky navigation bar to jump between topics
  • Installable on desktop, Android, and iPhone
  • Works offline once installed

How to Use the App

  1. Open the app using the link on the promo page
  2. Use the navigation bar at the top to jump to any topic
  3. Read the description on each card to learn about the wellness topic
  4. Press the play button on the audio player to listen to the audio guide
  5. Scroll through all 5 topics at your own pace

How to Install

On Chrome (Desktop or Android):

  1. Open the app in Google Chrome
  2. Click the Install button in the top right of the address bar
  3. Confirm the install prompt
  4. The app will appear on your desktop or home screen

On iPhone/iPad (Safari):

  1. Open the app in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen
  3. Select Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add to confirm
