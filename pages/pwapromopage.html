About WellnessGuide

WellnessGuide is a Progressive Web App designed to help you build healthy daily habits. Explore topics like Meditation, Journaling, Exercise, Third Spaces, and Hobbies where each have educational content and audio guidance. Install it on your device and access it anytime, even offline! Hope you enjoy and namaste.

How to Install

  1. Open the app in Google Chrome on your computer or Android device.
  2. Look for the Install button in the top right corner of the address bar.
  3. Click Install and confirm the prompt that appears.
  4. WellnessGuide will be added to your desktop or home screen.
  5. Open it anytime — it works offline too!
  6. On iPhone/iPad: tap the Share button then select Add to Home Screen.
