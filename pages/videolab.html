My Three Youtube Videos

PinkPantheress’s Beyoncé-Inspired Thin Brows Beauty Routine | Beauty Secrets | Vogue

Vogue’s youtube channel gives celebrities the platform to expose their unique beauty hacks and secrets. This video is guided by singer PinkPantheress, an up and coming popstar. Throughout the video she provides insight on her music career and makeup inspiration. Her distinctive, desired hack is her early 2000’s Beyonce inspired eyebrows which have sparked attention by her fans.

Zendaya & Robert Pattinson Ask Rapid-Fire Questions | Off The Cuff | Vogue

Vogue’s youtube channel also supplies celebrities a chance to promote their films and projects in playful ways. This video presents a rapid fire question game between Zendaya and Robert Patterson. The actors answer questions that give the audience perception on their opinions with certain topics that correlate with their new film, The Drama.

What happens to your brain without any social contact? - Terry Kupers

Ted-ed’s channel provides videos that teach people about emotions, trauma, mental health, and more. Although alone time is essential for our well being, too much of it can be detrimental. This video teaches people about isolation and how it affects the brain. Isolation can cause emotional instability, mental health issues, and depression. This video emphasizes how important social interactions are for our well being and to make sure we keep a balanced lifestyle.
